# Northstar Analytics — Employee Handbook (Sample)

## Working Model
Employees assigned to the Singapore office are expected to work from the office at least two days per week.

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried leave must be used by 31 March or it expires.

## Medical Leave
Employees receive 14 days of paid outpatient sick leave per calendar year after completing three months of service. A medical certificate is required when an employee is absent for more than two consecutive working days.

## Learning Budget
Permanent employees who have completed six months of service may claim up to SGD 1,200 per calendar year for approved professional courses, certifications, books, or conferences. Manager approval is required before purchase.

## Information Security
Employees must not share passwords or API keys in source code, chat messages, tickets, screenshots, or public repositories. Secrets must be stored in the approved secret-management solution or local environment variables for development.
