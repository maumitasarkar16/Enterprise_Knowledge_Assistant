# Northstar Analytics — Business Travel Policy (Sample)

## Booking
Business travel should be booked through the approved corporate travel portal. Air travel under six hours should normally be economy class. Premium economy may be approved for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires Vice President approval.

## Hotels
The standard hotel reimbursement cap is SGD 280 per night before taxes. A higher rate may be reimbursed when conference hotels, safety, accessibility, or local availability make the standard cap impractical, provided the employee obtains manager approval and documents the reason.

## Meals
Employees may claim actual meal expenses up to SGD 90 per day during approved business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Ground Transport
Public transport and standard taxi or ride-hailing fares are reimbursable when reasonably required for business travel. Luxury vehicle upgrades are not reimbursable.

## Receipts
Itemized receipts are required for individual expenses of SGD 25 or more.
