# Atlas Enterprise Platform — Support Guide (Sample)

## Support Tiers
Standard Support operates Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 Singapore Time, excluding Singapore public holidays. Premium Support provides 24x7 coverage for Severity 1 incidents.

## Severity 1
A Severity 1 incident means the production service is unavailable for most users and no reasonable workaround exists. The Premium Support initial response target for Severity 1 is 30 minutes.

## Severity 2
A Severity 2 incident is a major degradation affecting important functions, but the service remains partially usable or a workaround exists. The Premium Support initial response target for Severity 2 is 2 hours.

## Escalation
If a Severity 1 incident has no meaningful progress update for 60 minutes after acknowledgement, the customer may request escalation to the incident manager.

## Backups
Production application databases are backed up daily. The standard retention period is 35 days.
